### MEDICINE: PARACETAMOL (Generic)
- Purpose: Used to reduce fever and relieve mild to moderate pain.
- Standard Dosage: Adults can take one 500mg tablet every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
- Maximum Limit: Strict limit of 4,000mg (8 tablets of 500mg) in a 24-hour period.
- Common Side Effects: Nausea, skin rash.
- Critical Warning: Severe liver damage can occur if the maximum daily limit is exceeded. Do not combine with other medications containing paracetamol.

### MEDICINE: CROCIN (Brand Name)
- Core Ingredient: Contains Paracetamol (500mg per standard tablet).
- Purpose: Commonly used to reduce fever and relieve body pain.
- Patient Advice: Take after food to prevent mild stomach irritation. Drink plenty of water.
- Safety Warning: Since Crocin contains paracetamol, do not take it at the same time as generic Paracetamol or Dolo 650.

### MEDICINE: DOLO 650 (Brand Name)
- Core Ingredient: Contains a higher dose of Paracetamol (650mg per tablet).
- Purpose: Used for treating high fever, severe headaches, and body pain.
- Patient Advice: Take only when explicitly needed. 
- Safety Warning: Because this is a stronger 650mg dose, adults must not take more than 6 tablets in one day. Do not combine with Crocin or generic Paracetamol.
